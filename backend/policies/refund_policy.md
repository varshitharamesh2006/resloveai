# Refund Policy

## Refund Methods
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method whenever possible.
- If the original payment method is unavailable (e.g., expired card), refund is issued as store credit, which never expires.

## Refund Timing
- Once approved, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. Bank posting can take an additional 2-7 business days depending on the customer's bank.

## Automatic vs Manual Approval
- Refunds under **₹5,000** for return reasons of "defective", "wrong item", or "damaged" can be **auto-approved** by the support system without human review.
- Refunds **₹5,000 or greater**, regardless of reason, require **human agent approval** before processing.
- Any refund requested WITHOUT an associated return (e.g., "just refund me, I'll keep the item") requires **human agent approval**, regardless of amount.
- Partial refunds (e.g., missing item in a multi-item order) can be auto-approved if the partial amount is under ₹5,000.

## Fraud Signals (always escalate to human)
- More than 2 refund requests from the same customer in a 30-day period.
- Refund requested for an order that was marked "delivered" less than 1 hour ago (possible false claim).
- Customer requests refund to a different account/payment method than the original purchase.
