# Cancellation Policy

## Before Shipment
- Orders with status "processing" (not yet shipped) can be cancelled instantly by the system with a full automatic refund. No human approval needed.

## After Shipment
- Orders with status "in_transit" or "delivered" CANNOT be cancelled through the cancellation flow. The customer should be guided to the Return Policy instead once the order arrives, or contact support to attempt an intercept (requires human agent, low success rate).

## Cancellation Fees
- No cancellation fees apply at any stage supported by self-service cancellation.
