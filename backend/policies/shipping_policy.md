# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Standard Delivery
- Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on location.
- Delivery delays beyond the "expected_delivery_date" by more than 3 days qualify the customer for a ₹100 delivery-delay credit, auto-issued as store credit, no human approval needed.

## Lost in Transit
- If tracking shows no movement for 5+ days and the order is not delivered, it is classified as "possibly lost". These cases must be escalated to a human agent for carrier investigation. Do NOT promise a refund or replacement without human confirmation.

## Missing Items
- If a customer reports a missing item from a multi-item order that shows as fully delivered, this can be handled as a partial refund following the Refund Policy auto-approval rules, as long as it is the customer's first such report in 30 days.
